Cupboards A self-inquiry notebook

Understand what’s on your mind.

Begin with how things feel and what you’d like to understand. Open a cupboard to look at one part of it more closely, in your own words and a guided conversation.

Then try something useful in everyday life, and notice what changes. When an exploration has done its work, bring it to a close and keep what you’ve learned. There’s no daily streak, and no reason to keep checking in.
