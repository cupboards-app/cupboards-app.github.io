About Cupboards

Is Cupboards therapy?

No. Cupboards is a notebook for looking at your own patterns. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything, and it doesn’t replace professional care. Some people use it alongside therapy.

Looking at this is uncomfortable. Is that normal?

Often, yes. Your mind has habits that keep painful things out of view, and looking past them is frequently where you learn the most.

If it’s more than uncomfortable, stop. Talk to someone you trust or a professional. Crisis lines are listed in Settings → Crisis resources.

How often should I use it?

Whenever you have something you’d like to look at. There are no streaks to keep up, and nothing is lost if you take a break.

Is there a point where I’m done?

Yes. An exploration is meant to come to a close. When you’re ready, you can graduate from Cupboards, and what you kept stays in the app for you to read.

The AI said something that doesn’t fit me.

AI can be wrong. Take what’s useful and leave the rest. You can say that a reply missed, and keep going in your own words.

Your notebook

Who can read what I write?

Nobody at Cupboards. Your notebook is kept on your phone, and there’s no account. When Cupboards asks for an AI reply, your message and the notes it needs go to our provider, which doesn’t keep them after replying.

The privacy page has the details.

Can I lock it?

Yes. Settings → Lock asks for Face ID or your passcode each time Cupboards opens.

I’m getting a new phone. How do I keep my notebook?

If you set up the new phone from a backup of the old one, your notebook comes with it.

You can also move it yourself. On the old phone, use Settings → Data management → Export data and choose a password. On the new phone, use Import data and enter the same password. Importing replaces whatever notebook is already there.

Can I use it on an iPad, on Android, or on two phones?

Cupboards is for iPhone. For now, your notebook lives on one phone. Syncing between devices isn’t available yet. We’re working on a secure way to do it.

Does it work without internet?

You can read your notebook and write in it. AI replies need a connection.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data management → Delete all data erases the notebook from the app. Files you exported and backups of your phone are separate copies. See your privacy controls.

When something’s wrong

Something isn’t working.

If an AI reply doesn’t arrive, check your internet connection and try again in a little while. Your notebook is still there, and you can keep reading and writing in the meantime. Please don’t delete your notebook or reinstall the app to fix a problem.

If it keeps happening, email the address above with your iPhone model, iOS version, Cupboards version, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove personal writing and identifying details first. TestFlight testers can also use TestFlight’s feedback tools.

Can I start over with something new?

Use Settings → Start a new exploration. Your current exploration is archived, not deleted, and you choose a new focus.

Cupboards is for self-inquiry, not therapy, diagnosis or emergency care. AI can be wrong. This inbox is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. In the US, call or text 988 for crisis support. About the 988 Lifeline.
