Common questions

Do I need to set up AI?

No. AI replies are built in. There is no key to add and no account to make. Settings → How AI replies work shows what is sent with a request.

The privacy page says who receives it.

What if AI won’t respond?

Check your internet connection, then try again in a little while.

If it still fails, email the error message with private content removed. You can still read saved notes and write local entries without AI. Don’t delete your notebook or reinstall the app just to troubleshoot.

Can I turn AI off?

This version has no switch that turns AI off. Sending a message makes a request. So do AI-written openings, follow-up questions, summaries and practices, sometimes without a new message from you. A request already sent cannot be recalled.

Sharing sleep and exercise from Apple Health is separate. It is off unless you choose it, in Settings → Apple Health. See your privacy controls.

How do I save a copy or delete my notebook?

In Settings → Data management, use Export data for a password-protected export, or Delete all data to erase the app’s notebook records. Keep your export password somewhere safe. Importing an export replaces the current notebook, so export anything you want to keep first.

Exports and backups of your phone are separate copies. See your privacy controls.

Can I begin a different exploration?

Use Settings → Start a new exploration to archive the current exploration and find a new focus. Earlier reflections remain separate. Replay the introduction only shows the first screens again; it does not reset your notebook.

How do I report a problem?

Email the support address above with your iPhone model, iOS version, Cupboards version, what you tried and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove personal writing and identifying details first. TestFlight testers can also use TestFlight’s feedback tools.

Cupboards is for self-inquiry, not therapy, diagnosis or emergency care. AI can be wrong. This inbox is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. In the US, call or text 988 for crisis support. About the 988 Lifeline.
